## 📚 ABOUT THE ACTIVITY

### 📌 Activity Title
**Google Sheets: Exploring Digital Spreadsheets and Collaboration**

### 📝 Short Description

This activity was a group presentation focused on exploring the **features, functions, and real-life applications of Google Sheets**, a web-based spreadsheet application developed by Google.

As **Group 1**, we prepared a creative and informative presentation explaining how Google Sheets can be used to organize information, perform calculations, manage budgets, and collaborate with others in real time.

Our presentation included demonstrations of basic spreadsheet formulas, such as **SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT, IF, MIN, MAX, and RANK**, along with practical examples of how Google Sheets can be applied in school, business, healthcare, research, and personal budgeting.

We also introduced an interactive activity called **"Budget or Burado?"**, where participants determined whether expenses were within or beyond a given budget. This made the discussion more engaging while helping everyone understand the importance of budgeting and spreadsheet calculations.

Through this activity, I learned how to use **Google Sheets to organize information, perform calculations, and manage data efficiently**.
