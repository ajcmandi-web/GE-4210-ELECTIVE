## 🌍 ABOUT THE ACTIVITY

### 📌 Activity Title
**EcoWatt: Smart Energy Monitoring System**

### 📝 Short Description

This activity focused on developing an innovative technology-based concept called **EcoWatt**, a smart energy monitoring system designed to help schools and organizations monitor their electricity consumption.

The project aimed to address the problem of **high electricity consumption, increasing electricity bills, and unnecessary energy waste** by introducing a system that provides real-time monitoring, usage reports, estimated electricity costs, and high-energy consumption alerts.

As part of the activity, I created an infographic presenting the problem, proposed solution, system features, benefits, and environmental impact of EcoWatt.

Through this project, I explored how technology and creative problem-solving can contribute to energy conservation and environmental sustainability.

---

## ⚡ KEY FEATURES OF ECOWATT

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| 📊 Real-Time Monitoring | Tracks electricity consumption in real time. |
| 🔔 High-Usage Alerts | Notifies users when electricity consumption becomes unusually high. |
| 🏫 Room/Device Tracking | Monitors energy consumption in different areas. |
| 📈 Usage Reports | Provides daily, weekly, and monthly electricity usage reports. |
| 💰 Cost Estimation | Displays estimated electricity expenses. |
| 🌿 Energy-Saving Tips | Provides recommendations to encourage energy conservation. |

## 🌱 WHAT HAVE I LEARNED?

Through this activity, I learned how **technology can be used to address real-world problems**, particularly excessive electricity consumption and environmental concerns. I also gained a better understanding of how smart monitoring systems can help individuals and organizations become more aware of their energy usage and make responsible decisions.

One challenge I faced was organizing the different features and processes of EcoWatt into an infographic that was both informative and easy to understand. I addressed this by dividing the information into clear sections, using illustrations, and presenting the system's functions through a simple step-by-step process.

This activity helped me improve my **creativity, critical thinking, problem-solving, and visual communication skills**. In the future, I can apply these skills when developing technology-based solutions, creating informative presentations, and promoting sustainable practices in my school and community.
