# Activity 3: EcoWatt - Smart Energy Monitoring System

## Inspiration / Problem

This activity focuses on designing an infographic for **EcoWatt: Smart Energy Monitoring System**, a concept created from a personal experience.

### Personal Inspiration
- Inspired by living in a dormitory and experiencing unexpectedly high electricity bills.
- Recognized a common problem where users are often unaware of which appliances or activities consume the most electricity.

### Problem Identified
- People usually only discover their electricity consumption after receiving their monthly bill.
- Users lack real-time information about their energy usage and spending habits.

---

## Main Idea

The concept was inspired by the question:

> "What if we could see our electricity consumption before receiving the bill?"

The goal was to create a system that allows users to understand and manage their energy consumption before costs become a problem.

---

## Proposed Solution

Created:

# EcoWatt: Smart Energy Monitoring System

EcoWatt is a smart energy monitoring system designed to help users track, understand, and reduce unnecessary electricity consumption.

---

## How EcoWatt Works

EcoWatt uses smart technology to collect and analyze electricity usage data.

### System Process

1. **Smart meters and sensors**
   - Detect and measure electricity consumption from appliances and activities.

2. **Data collection**
   - Gather real-time energy usage information.

3. **Digital dashboard**
   - Displays energy consumption data in a simple and understandable format.

---

## Key Features

Users can monitor:

### Current Power Consumption
- Shows real-time electricity usage.

### Estimated Electricity Cost
- Provides an estimated amount of energy expenses based on current usage.

### Usage Patterns
- Helps users identify their daily or weekly electricity habits.

### High Energy Consumption Areas
- Highlights appliances or activities that use excessive electricity.

---

## Alert System

EcoWatt includes an alert system that notifies users when energy consumption becomes unusually high.

Users can check possible causes such as:

- Lights left on unnecessarily
- Air-conditioning units running for extended periods
- Computers or electronic devices consuming power
- Other high-energy appliances

---

## Infographic Organization

The information was structured into clear sections:

1. **The Problem**
   - Identifies the issue of unknown electricity consumption.

2. **Why It Happens**
   - Explains the lack of real-time energy monitoring.

3. **The Solution**
   - Introduces EcoWatt as the proposed system.

4. **How EcoWatt Works**
   - Explains the process of data collection and monitoring.

5. **Key Features**
   - Highlights the capabilities of the system.

6. **Benefits**
   - Shows how users can improve energy awareness.

7. **Impact**
   - Demonstrates how technology can encourage smarter energy use.

---

## Design Goal

The main objective was to transform a technical concept into an infographic that is:

- Simple
- Understandable
- Accessible to ordinary users

The design focuses on presenting complex technology in a way that can be easily understood by a general audience.

---

## Key Learning

Through this activity, I learned that design is not only about creating visually appealing materials but also about solving problems and communicating ideas effectively.

Good design combined with technology can help people:

- Understand their energy habits
- Make better decisions
- Reduce unnecessary energy consumption
- Develop more sustainable practices

---

## Conclusion

EcoWatt demonstrates how design and technology can work together to address everyday problems. By making energy consumption visible and understandable, users can become more aware of their habits and take action toward more efficient energy use.
