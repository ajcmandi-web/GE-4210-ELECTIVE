## 🌿 01. About the Activity

> "One prompt can lead to different possibilities."

This laboratory activity explores how different **Large Language Models (LLMs)** generate content when given identical instructions.

The task involves creating a **one-page professional resume** using at least two AI platforms, then comparing their outputs based on structure, clarity, formatting, and overall quality.

For this project, I used **ChatGPT** and **Google Gemini** to create resumes for an entry-level staff nurse application.

Although both tools received the same information and instructions, they produced different designs and presentations.

---

## 🎯 02. Objectives

The activity aims to:

1. Explore the content-generation capabilities of different Large Language Models.
2. Generate a one-page professional resume using the same prompt on two AI platforms.
3. Compare how ChatGPT and Gemini organize and present identical information.
4. Evaluate each resume based on structure, clarity, formatting, and overall quality.
5. Identify the strengths and limitations of each AI-generated output.
6. Reflect on the importance of human evaluation when using AI tools.

### The Process

**STEP 01 — Prepare the Prompt**

A single prompt was prepared containing the applicant's professional summary, educational background, clinical skills, certifications, and clinical experience.

**STEP 02 — Generate the Resumes**

The identical prompt was submitted to ChatGPT and Google Gemini.

**STEP 03 — Examine the Outputs**

Both AI-generated resumes were reviewed based on their design, organization, readability, and presentation.

**STEP 04 — Compare the Results**

A comparison table was created to identify the similarities and differences between the two outputs.

**STEP 05 — Reflect**

The activity concluded with a personal reflection on the effectiveness of both tools.

---

## 🤖 04. The AI Platforms

| 🟢 ChatGPT | 🔵 Google Gemini |
|:---|:---|
| Creative and modern design | Simple and minimalist design |
| Two-column layout | Single-column layout |
| Green color palette | Blue border |
| Icons and profile image | Bold headings |
| Personalized presentation | Straightforward organization |

Both platforms included the essential professional information but differed in how they presented it.

---

## ⚖️ 05. Comparison of Results

| Criteria | ChatGPT | Gemini |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Structure** | Organized two-column layout | Straightforward single-column layout |
| **Clarity** | Visually appealing, but some text was small | Clear and easy to read |
| **Formatting** | Green headings, icons, and profile image | Blue border and consistent text |
| **Overall Quality** | Creative and personalized | Clean and professional |

### 🏆 Preferred Output: ChatGPT

ChatGPT was my preferred output because its design appeared more creative, personalized, and visually engaging.

However, Gemini demonstrated the value of simplicity and readability in professional resume preparation.

---

## 💡 06. Key Findings

### 🎨 Creativity vs. Simplicity

ChatGPT emphasized design and visual appeal, while Gemini focused on clean formatting and readability.

### 📄 Same Content, Different Presentation

Both AI tools included similar professional information, showing that identical prompts can produce different layouts and styles.

### 🧠 Human Judgment Matters

AI-generated documents still require human review to ensure that the final resume is accurate, readable, and appropriate for professional applications.

---

## 📝 07. Personal Reflection

This laboratory activity helped me understand how different Large Language Models interpret and respond to the same instructions.

Although ChatGPT and Gemini received identical prompts, their outputs differed in layout, formatting, and overall presentation.

I preferred ChatGPT's resume because its green color palette, icons, profile picture, and organized sections created a more personalized design suitable for a nursing applicant.

On the other hand, Gemini produced a simpler resume that was easier to follow and maintained a professional appearance.

Through this activity, I realized that an effective resume requires more than complete information. It also needs proper organization, readability, and a professional design.

Most importantly, I learned that artificial intelligence can assist in creating professional documents, but the final output should always be evaluated carefully.
