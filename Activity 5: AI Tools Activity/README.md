## 🤖 ABOUT THE ACTIVITY

### 📌 Activity Title
**Exploring and Improving AI Prompts**

### 📝 Short Description

This activity focused on understanding how **different prompts influence the images generated by Artificial Intelligence (AI)**.

Using **ChatGPT**, I experimented with two prompts to generate images of a school. The first prompt was simple and general, while the second provided more specific details about the subject, location, appearance, and activity.

The activity allowed me to compare both AI-generated images and observe how the amount of information included in a prompt affects the final output.

Through this experience, I explored the importance of **prompt engineering, creativity, and effective communication with AI tools**.

---

## 🎨 PROMPT EXPERIMENT

### 🏫 FIRST PROMPT: SIMPLE INSTRUCTIONS

**Prompt Used:**
> "Make me a photo of a school."

**Generated Result:**

The AI generated an image of a modern school building featuring a clean entrance, green trees, a wide driveway, and a bright blue sky.

The image showed a peaceful, organized, and welcoming school environment.

### 🎒 SECOND PROMPT: DETAILED INSTRUCTIONS

**Prompt Used:**
> "Make me a photo of a beautiful girl entering Ateneo de Davao School in school uniform with her Birkin bag checked by a guard. Brown hair, light to medium skin tone."

**Generated Result:**

The AI generated a more detailed school entrance scene featuring:

- 👩 A female student with brown hair
- 👕 A school uniform
- 👜 A Birkin-style bag
- 🏫 An Ateneo de Davao University entrance
- 👮 A security guard checking the student's bag
- 🌳 Trees and buildings surrounding the campus

The second image followed a more specific scenario compared with the first.

---

## 📊 COMPARISON OF AI PROMPTS

| Category | First Prompt | Second Prompt |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| 📝 Instructions | Simple and general | Detailed and specific |
| 🏫 Main Subject | School building | Student entering school |
| 🎨 Visual Details | Minimal | More descriptive |
| 📍 Location | Unspecified school | Ateneo de Davao |
| 🤖 AI Interpretation | More creative freedom | More guided output |
| 🎯 Result | General school image | Specific school scenario |

**Key Observation:**

The second prompt produced an image that more closely followed my intended idea because it included clearer instructions and specific visual details.

## 🌱 WHAT HAVE I LEARNED?

Through this activity, I learned that **writing clear and detailed prompts is important when using Artificial Intelligence**, especially when generating images. I discovered that simple prompts give AI more freedom to interpret instructions, while specific prompts help produce results that are closer to what I imagine.

One challenge I faced was figuring out how to describe the exact image I wanted the AI to generate. I addressed this by adding more details about the subject, appearance, location, clothing, and actions in my second prompt.

This activity helped me improve my **creativity, critical thinking, and ability to communicate instructions effectively**. In the future, I can apply these skills when using AI tools for school projects, presentations, digital designs, and other creative activities.
